Brazilian Feijoada

Description

This feijoada recipe is my version of a traditional Brazilian black bean stew that maintains the rich flavors famous in Brazil. Excellent served over brown rice. Additional meats, including pork loin or smoked sausage, may be added if desired.

Ingredients

  • 1 clove garlic, chopped
  • 1 (12ounce) package dry black beans, soaked overnight
  • 2 smoked ham hocks
  • 8 ounces duced ham
  • 1/2 pound thickly sliced bacon, diced
  • 2 bay leaves, crushed
  • 1/8 teaspoon ground coriander
  • salt and pepper to taste
  • 1/2 cup chopped fresh cilantro (Optional)
  • 1/4 cup chopped fresh parsley(Optional)
